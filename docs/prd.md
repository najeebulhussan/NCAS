# NajeebCyber AI Studio (NCAS) - Project Requirements Document (PRD)

**Project Name:** NajeebCyber AI Studio (NCAS)  
**Version:** 1.0 – AI-Powered Cyber Media Company  
**Owner / Editor-in-Chief:** Najeeb (@NajeebCyber)  
**Status:** Active Development  

---

## 1. Vision & Mission Statement

### Mission
> Build the world's first AI-driven cybersecurity news studio focused on delivering fast, accurate, visually compelling cyber news, threat intelligence, and awareness content across multiple video and digital platforms.

### Core Objectives
1. **Autonomous Intelligence**: Continuously monitor global cybersecurity news sources (The Hacker News, Wired Security, BleepingComputer, CISA alerts, WEF Cyber, GitHub/arXiv).
2. **Speed-to-Broadcast**: Transform breaking threat alerts into studio-grade 60-second video broadcasts in under 15 minutes.
3. **High Engagement & Trust**: Combine cinematic newsroom aesthetics (blue neon, floating holograms, attack heatmaps) with verified facts and actionable advice.
4. **Omnichannel Growth**: Dominate short-form video algorithms (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels) and professional networks (LinkedIn, X, Facebook).

---

## 2. Platform Roadmap & Phases

### Phase 1: Foundation & Core Content Engine (Current)
- Establish unified branding across all social handles (\`@NajeebCyber\`).
- Implement 12 specialized AI Subagent roles for news discovery, scriptwriting, visual asset creation, and quality assurance.
- Deploy Master OmniFlash Prompt (v2.0) for automated video script generation.
- Produce 1-Minute Weekly Cyber News anchor broadcasts.

### Phase 2: Full Autonomous AI Newsroom
- Integration with live RSS feeds, Google News API, and OSINT threat monitors.
- Automated B-roll generation and AI Avatar rendering (HeyGen / Midjourney / ElevenLabs / Google Flow).
- Multi-language captioning and automatic translation (English, Urdu/Hindi, Spanish).
- Automated posting and analytics feedback loop.

---

## 3. Key Target Audiences
1. **IT Professionals & Security Analysts (CISO, SOC Engineers)**: Seek fast, high-level summaries of zero-day exploits and patch recommendations.
2. **Tech Enthusiasts & AI Developers**: Seek visual breakdowns of how AI attacks or defenses work in real-time.
3. **General Public & Everyday Users**: Seek simple, jargon-free security awareness ("Should You Care?" / "Security Tip of the Week").

---

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Watch Time & Retention**: >70% average retention on 60-second shorts.
- **Publishing Velocity**: 1 Daily Cyber News Short + 1 Weekly Recap Broadcast.
- **Engagement Rate**: >5% like-to-view ratio across YouTube Shorts and TikTok.
- **Brand Consistency**: 100% unified visual theme and color palette (Deep Cyber Blue #0A192F, Cyan Glow #00F0FF, Alert Red #FF0055).
