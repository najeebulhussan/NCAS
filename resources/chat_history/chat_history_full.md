### **Story Workspace**

Instead of treating content as isolated prompts, every news story becomes a persistent **Story Workspace** with its own lifecycle.

A Story Workspace would contain:
- Source articles and references
- Verification notes
- Research summary
- Script versions
- Google Flow prompt
- NanoBanana image prompts
- Thumbnail concepts
- SEO package
- Social media variants
- Approval history
- Performance metrics after publishing

Everything related to that story lives in one place from discovery through post-publication analysis.

This makes collaboration, versioning, reuse, and future updates much easier than rebuilding content from scratch each time. It also gives the platform a solid foundation for scaling into a professional editorial system.

# 📌 One Recommendation Before We Continue

Rather than building this as a collection of independent agents, I recommend designing it around a **central orchestration engine**.

That engine should:
- Maintain the state of every story from discovery to publication.
- Coordinate which agent runs next.
- Handle retries and errors.
- Track approvals.
- Record every action for auditing and future improvement.

This architecture is more scalable than agents talking directly to one another and will make future features—such as multilingual output, website publishing, or additional AI providers—much easier to add. It also gives you a clear, reliable workflow that matches how professional editorial systems operate.
